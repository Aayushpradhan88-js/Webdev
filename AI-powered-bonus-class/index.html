About Us

25 Years of Academic Excellence

Founded in 2056 BS (2001 AD), Everest Academy is one of Kathmandu's most trusted higher secondary and undergraduate institutions. Affiliated to Tribhuvan University and NEB, we have nurtured over 12,000 graduates who serve in sectors from medicine to engineering, banking to civil service.

Our campus spans 5 ropani in the heart of Kathmandu, with state-of-the-art labs, a 50,000-volume library, and dedicated sports facilities.
